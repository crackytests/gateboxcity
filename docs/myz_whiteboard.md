# Mutant: Year Zero — Game Reference Whiteboard
*Extracted from the 268-page Core Rulebook. For use in Redot/Godot 4 cyberpunk-adjacent project.*
*Cross-reference: iz2_whiteboard.md (Interface Zero 2.0), jd_whiteboard.md (Judge Dredd)*

## SECTION 1: Setting Tone & World

**The Hook**: The world ended. Red Plague hit an already-collapsing civilization. Nuclear war followed. Now Earth is a wasteland called the Zone. Small groups of mutants — the People — live in isolated settlements called Arks. No one is over 30. No children are born. Extinction is a math problem.

**Year Zero**: The apocalypse itself. The name implies this is the start of something, not just the end.

**Tone registers**:
- **Survival horror**: grub, water, bullets are currency. Starvation is always one session away.
- **Folk mythology**: the Ancients (pre-apocalypse people), the Elder (dying guardian), Eden (legendary salvation). Everything is known second-hand, filtered through generations of mutation and ignorance.
- **Political drama**: the Ark is a tiny society tearing itself apart — Bosses extort, Slaves rebel, Chroniclers agitate.
- **Exploration wonder**: the Zone is alien and beautiful and lethal. Every ruin is a window into a forgotten world.
- **Existential dread**: the People can't have children. If they do nothing, they die. The question is what they build before that happens.

**The Big Twist** (GM-only metaplot): The People aren't survivors of the apocalypse. They are **genetically engineered test subjects**, created after the Fall by scientists of the Mimir enclave as **Project Eden** — an attempt to build a new, non-aggressive human race. Their Ark was a controlled experiment. The Elder is **Dr. Sebedius**, one of the original researchers, who has kept quiet on orders for decades. "Eden" is not a myth — it's the abandoned Project Eden research command center, reachable via three artifact clues.

**The Titan Powers**: Warring alliances of space/underground enclaves (the surviving rich who fled Earth). Still at war far above and below the mutants' heads. Will matter in expansions. The Enclave War is ongoing.

**Usable for our game**: The "you don't know your own origin" premise maps perfectly to corporate/government conspiracies in our cyberpunk setting. The Dawn Vault = information-hoarding institution. Ark projects = faction development arc. The Elder as a dying information node who knows the truth.

## SECTION 2: The Ark

**What it is**: A small settlement (~200 people) built on or inside ruins of the Old Age. The starting point of every campaign. All PCs live here.

**Ark type options** (choose flavor):
- Airplane Wreck, Bridge, Farm, Gated Community, Junkyard, **Mall**, Oil Rig, Prison, Scrap Village, Shelter, Shipwreck, Skyscraper, Small Island, Subway Station, Trailer Park

→ **MALL IS EXPLICITLY LISTED**. Our hub is a canonical Ark type.

**The Dawn Vault**: A sacred archive where artifacts from the Old Age are supposed to be deposited for the good of all. Guarded by loyal Chroniclers. Mutants increasingly keep artifacts for themselves instead. Tension between personal power and collective benefit. Handing in an artifact = DEV bonus for the Ark. Keeping it = personal power but bad optics.

**The Elder**: Near-death figurehead. Was once the community's teacher and protector. Now bedridden. His commands still influence the Ark even as he fades. The GM uses him as a slow-release clue dispenser who can't be forced.

**Session Structure**:
1. Each session starts with an **Assembly** — players vote on which Ark Projects to pursue
2. GM introduces a threat (from Threat Cards or roll)
3. Play out the session
4. End: XP award based on personal goals

**The Assembly**: Players step out of character and collectively decide Ark development. Democratic structure even if individual characters are dictators. Very useful for player agency in faction decisions.

**Population Management**: D6 people die per session from violence, hunger, Rot (Session Body Count). Higher Food Supply DEV reduces this. No children are ever born. Population is a countdown timer unless outsiders move in or the truth about Eden is discovered.

## SECTION 3: Development Levels (DEVs)

Four DEV tracks, each scored 0–60+. Ark Projects advance them. Artifacts deposited in the Dawn Vault also advance them.

| DEV | What it governs |
|-----|----------------|
| **Food Supply** | Hunger severity, Session Body Count reduction |
| **Culture** | Reading/writing, understanding artifacts, art forms |
| **Technology** | Using/repairing artifacts, technical innovations |
| **Warfare** | Defense strength (Battle Level), militia, military capacity |

**Artifact DEV gates**: Each artifact has a DEV Requirement — if the Ark's DEV is below that number, the People can't use the artifact without rolling Comprehend. High Culture = more Old Age knowledge accessible.

**Key DEV thresholds** (narrative flavor):
- Culture 0-9: Only Chroniclers can read/write. No cultural expression beyond campfire stories.
- Culture 10-19: Basic literacy. Simple performances, exhibitions.
- Culture 30+: Extended suffrage, inclusive governance.
- Technology 0-9: Nobody understands technology except Gearheads.
- Technology 50: Radio communication becomes possible.

## SECTION 4: Ark Projects (Complete List)

Projects are the Ark's "tech tree." Players vote on them at Assembly. Each has a DEV Requirement, skill used, Work Points cost, and DEV Bonus.

**Tier 0 (No DEV requirement)**:
- Defenses (palisade/moat) → Warfare +2D6
- Hunting Party → Food +2D6
- Cannibalism → Food +3D6, Culture -D6, Body Count +D6 (desperate measure)
- Cropland → Food +3D6
- Slave Market → Food +D6, Warfare +D6
- Pigsty → Food +2D6
- Temple → Culture +D6
- Zone Wrestling (cage fights) → Warfare +D6
- Zone Expedition → variable

**Tier 1 (DEV 5-10)**:
- Museum (Culture 5) → Culture +D6, Technology +D6
- Statue (Culture 5) → Culture +D6
- Sundial (Culture 5) → Technology +D6
- Watchtower (Technology 5) → Warfare +D6
- Harbor (Technology 10, needs water) → enables Ship
- Mill (Technology 10 + Cropland) → Food +2D6
- Smokehouse (Food 5, Tech 10 + Hunting Party) → Food +D6
- **Marketplace** (Food 10, Culture 10) → Food +D6, Culture +D6
- Militia (Warfare 10) → Warfare +2D6
- Library (Culture 10) → Culture +3D6
- Stables (Food 10) → enables mounts, Food +D6, Warfare +D6
- **Tavern** (Food 10) → Culture +D6
- Theater (Culture 10) → Culture +2D6
- Torture Chamber (Warfare 10, Culture 10) → Warfare +D6
- Tribunal (Warfare 10, Culture 10) → Culture +D6, Warfare +D6
- Water Wheel (Technology 10) → Technology +D6
- **Workshop** (Technology 10) → Technology +2D6, enables other projects

**Tier 2 (DEV 15-20)**:
- Calendar (Tech 15, Culture 10) → Culture +D6
- Windmill (Tech 15, Food 10) → Technology +D6
- Ink & Paper (Tech 15, Culture 10) → Culture +D6
- Army (Warfare 20) → Warfare +2D6
- Bank (Warfare 10, Culture 20) → Culture +D6
- Ship (Technology 20 + Harbor) → exploration, Food/Culture/Warfare +D6
- Generator (Technology 20 + Workshop) → Technology +2D6
- Heliograph (Technology 20 + Workshop + contact) → Culture +D6
- School (Culture 20) → Culture +D6, Technology +D6
- Settlement (Warfare 20, Culture 20) → new outpost
- Road (Technology 20, per sector) → Food +D6, Culture +D6
- Printing Press (Tech 20, Culture 20) → Culture +2D6

**Tier 3 (DEV 25-35)**:
- Foundry (Technology 25 + Workshop) → enables advanced projects
- Explosives Lab (Tech 30, Warfare 10) → Warfare +D6, enables bullet manufacturing
- **Autocracy** (Warfare 30) — one Boss rules all → Warfare +2D6 (conflicts with Suffrage)
- **Free Enterprise** (Technology 30) — slavery outlawed → Technology +2D6 (conflicts with Collectivism)
- **Collectivism** (Food 30) — no private ownership → Food +2D6 (conflicts with Free Enterprise)
- **Suffrage** (Culture 30) — universal vote → Technology +2D6 (conflicts with Autocracy)
- Steam Engine (Tech 30 + Foundry + Distillery) → Technology +2D6, powers Generator/Ship/Train
- Railroad (Tech 35 + Foundry, per sector) → enables Train

**Tier 4 (DEV 35-50+)**:
- Arsenal (Tech 35, Warfare 10 + Foundry) → Warfare +2D6, enables breech-loaders
- Surgery (Tech 35 + Distillery) → Heal +2 mod, Body Count -1
- Electric Lights (Tech 40 + Generator) → Technology +2D6
- Train (Tech 30 + Steam Engine + Railroad) → massive transport
- Radio (Tech 50 + Generator + Workshop) → Technology +2D6

**Notes for our game**: Marketplace, Tavern, Theater, Library, Museum, Bank are all "hub-building" projects — perfect for a mall hub narrative arc. Autocracy/Suffrage/Collectivism/Free Enterprise are governance unlock options that can shape faction relationships dramatically.

## SECTION 5: Player Roles (NPC Archetypes)

Eight roles. Each has: appearance options, key attribute, specialist skill, three starting talents, relationship seeds to other PCs and NPCs, big dream options, and starting gear. Excellent NPC design templates.

### ENFORCER
**Role**: Pure fighter. Survived by crushing everything.
**Key Attribute**: Strength | **Specialist Skill**: Intimidate
**Talents**: Barge Through, Mean Streak, Sucker Punch
**Appearance**: broken nose, welding mask, hockey mask, metal jaw / scarred/muscular/huge / worn leather coat, cut-up car tires, hubcaps
**Dreams**: To crush everything pretty. For beloved to love them back. To build rather than destroy.
**NPC seeds**: Boss Johammed (enemy), Enforcer Elon (rival who beat them), Fixer Milix (stole their grub)
**Starting gear**: D6 bullets, 2D6 grub, D6 water + spiked bat OR brass knuckles OR scrap axe

### GEARHEAD
**Role**: Inventor, scavenger, engineer. The Zone is their hardware store.
**Key Attribute**: Wits | **Specialist Skill**: Jury-Rig
**Talents**: Inventor (+2 to build), Motorhead (+1 vehicles), Tinkerer (+2 to repair)
**Appearance**: goggles, always chewing, spiked hair, dirty yellow coverall, cables and light bulbs
**Dreams**: Build something that changes the People. Find Eden's tech marvels. Build a weapon of mass destruction.
**Starting gear**: 2D6 bullets, D6 grub, D6 water, 1 random artifact + scrap pistol/knuckles/bicycle chain

### STALKER
**Role**: Zone explorer. Feared and needed. Only one who knows the safe paths.
**Key Attribute**: Agility | **Specialist Skill**: Find the Path
**Talents**: Monster Hunter (+2 Scout beasts), Rot Finder (assess Rot levels), Scavenger (+2 find artifacts)
**Appearance**: hidden under hood, sickly pale, bandaged / wiry or muscular / raincoat, camouflage gear, army boots, backpack
**Dreams**: Venture deep into Zone and find Eden. Stop wandering and find peace. Find something worth dying for.
**Starting gear**: D6 bullets, D6 grub, 2D6 water + scrap rifle OR scrap pistol OR bow

### FIXER
**Role**: Dealer, negotiator, information broker. Knows what everyone needs and how to get it.
**Key Attribute**: Empathy | **Specialist Skill**: Make a Deal
**Talents**: Wheeler Dealer (double earnings), Vicious Creep (+1 doubt from Manipulate), Juicy Info (blackmail dirt on one NPC)
**Appearance**: always smiling, unnaturally attractive, greasy / slender, no legs / suit, leather coat, hat, gloves
**Dreams**: Make a deal worth a lifetime of bullets. Become a Boss with their own gang.
**Starting gear**: 2D6 bullets, 2D6 grub, D6 water + scrap knife OR brass knuckles OR scrap derringer

### DOG HANDLER
**Role**: Bonded to their mutant dog. Symbiotic defender.
**Key Attribute**: Agility | **Specialist Skill**: Sic a Dog
**Talents**: Bloodhound (+2 tracking), Fight Dog (+2 fighting), Mutant's Best Friend (dog assists Endure/Move)
**Appearance**: hairless, unbinking, disfigured / wiry, scrawny, hunched / overalls, hoodie, heavy boots
**Dog stats**: Strength 4, Agility 6. Can fight, scout, and track. Dies if Strength reaches 0.
**Starting gear**: D6 bullets, D6 grub, D6 water + bicycle chain OR scrap knife OR slingshot OR scrap rifle

### CHRONICLER
**Role**: Scholar, historian, bard, healer. Keeper of memory.
**Key Attribute**: Empathy | **Specialist Skill**: Inspire
**Talents**: Agitator (+2 Inspire others to fight), Bonesaw (+2 Heal critical injuries), Performer (earn bullets/grub by performing)
**Appearance**: sickly pale, hairless head, serious / skinny, hunchback, gnarled / worn coverall with number, covered in Old Age paraphernalia
**Special**: Can read and write even when Ark Culture DEV is 0. Unique literacy edge.
**Dreams**: Ark becomes a place of culture and learning. Write the epic of the People finding Eden. Bring children to the People.
**Starting gear**: D6 bullets, D6 grub, D6 water (no free weapon)

### BOSS
**Role**: Gang leader. Leads through manipulation, intrigue, and naked violence.
**Key Attribute**: Wits | **Specialist Skill**: Command
**Talents**: Commander (+2 Command to fight), Gunslingers (gang all have scrap guns), Racketeer (+2 extortion rolls)
**Appearance**: moon face, watery eyes, no legs (needs carrying) / standing upright or short / suit, fur coat, jewelry
**Gang mechanics**: Skill level = gang size (~3-4 per level). Gang composition: mostly Enforcers. Racketeering = extort D6 bullets/grub/water per session.
**Dreams**: Eliminate all rivals and rule the Ark. Build a new civilization. Finally be free of all responsibilities.
**Starting gear**: 2D6 bullets, 2D6 grub, D6 water + spiked bat OR scrap knife OR scrap pistol

### SLAVE
**Role**: The bottom. Endures everything. Dreams of rebellion or escape.
**Key Attribute**: Strength | **Specialist Skill**: Shake it Off (reduce any trauma)
**Talents**: Cynic (+2 Endure against doubt), Rebel (Endure successes become action bonuses), Resilient (+2 Endure against damage)
**Appearance**: expressionless, dead eyes, powerful or skinny / chains, torn T-shirt, dirty coverall
**Dreams**: Rebel, kill Bosses, free all Slaves. Please their owner. Escape and find a better life.
**Starting gear**: 0 bullets, D6 grub, D6 water + spiked bat OR bicycle chain OR slingshot

## SECTION 6: Skills & Dice System

**The Core Mechanic**: Roll dice, need at least one success (/). Extra successes = stunts.
- **Base Dice** (yellow) = current attribute score
- **Skill Dice** (green) = skill level (0-5)
- **Gear Dice** (black) = item's Gear Bonus

**Pushing**: Reroll all dice that didn't show /, =, or gear-fail symbol. Every = symbol rolled (across both rolls) = 1 trauma to that attribute AND 1 Mutation Point gained. High risk, high reward.

**Modifications**: Difficulty table from +3 (A Breeze) to -3 (Insane). Help from others = +1 per helper (max +3). Specialist skills cannot receive help.

### 12 Basic Skills

| Skill | Attribute | Key Uses |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Endure | Strength | Survive harsh conditions, over-encumbrance |
| Force | Strength | Lift, push, smash barriers |
| Fight | Strength | Close combat attack and defense |
| Sneak | Agility | Avoid detection, set ambushes |
| Move | Agility | Escape conflicts, acrobatics, climbing |
| Shoot | Agility | Ranged combat |
| Scout | Wits | Spot threats/details at a distance |
| Comprehend | Wits | Read Old Age text, understand artifacts |
| Know the Zone | Wits | Identify Zone threats and phenomena |
| Sense Emotion | Empathy | Read NPC mood and intent |
| Manipulate | Empathy | Convince, threaten, seduce, deceive |
| Heal | Empathy | Stabilize the broken, treat critical injuries |

### Specialist Skills Summary

| Role | Skill | Core Function |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Enforcer | Intimidate | Physical threat that forces compliance or attack |
| Gearhead | Jury-Rig | Build devices, repair gear, modify vehicles |
| Stalker | Find the Path | Navigate Zone safely, find artifacts/resources |
| Fixer | Make a Deal | Locate contacts, earn resources on the side |
| Dog Handler | Sic a Dog | Dog fights, scouts, tracks for you |
| Chronicler | Inspire | Help/hinder others' skill rolls by giving them your successes |
| Boss | Command | Order your gang, extort resources, lead battles |
| Slave | Shake it Off | Reduce trauma of any type (except from pushing) |

**Jury-Rig buildable devices** (from scrap):
- Scrap gun, arrows (×4), scrap armor (AR3), shield (AR3)
- Rot suit (Protection 3 vs Rot), cold suit (+3 Endure cold)
- Explosive charge (Blast Power 6), flaming torch, lamp
- Binoculars (+1 Scout), foghorn, destillator (D6 booze doses)
- Camouflage (+2 Sneak), scrap raft (5 people)
- Cart (cargo), balloon (one person, limited flight)
- Scrap cannon (explosive charge per shot)

## SECTION 7: Mutations

**Activation**: Spend Mutation Points (MP). Cannot fail — but can **misfire** (roll one Base Die per MP spent; any = = misfire). Misfire table: permanent trauma + new mutation / self-damage / double MP cost / mutation locked for session / appearance change.

**Permanent Trauma**: Attributes permanently reduced. This is what it means to be a mutant. Slowly degenerate and gain more powers as you go.

**Starting MP**: 1 MP per mutation at session start. Pushed rolls generate more MP (=s rolled). Max 10 MP.

**New Mutations**: When a mutation misfires with a 1 (=), gain a new mutation (permanently). Max 4 mutations. Each new mutation costs a permanent attribute point.

## SECTION 8: Conflict & Trauma

**Trauma Types** (each linked to attribute and recovery):
| Trauma | Attribute Reduced | Recovery |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| Damage | Strength | Grub |
| Fatigue | Agility | Water |
| Confusion | Wits | Sleep |
| Doubt | Empathy | Sympathy (another mutant's company) |

**Broken**: Attribute at 0 = broken, can't act. Another PC must Heal to restore. If untreated after a session → Critical Injury roll (can be permanent or fatal).

**Conditions**: Starving, Dehydrated, Sleepless, Hypothermic — each gives penalties until the corresponding resource is consumed.

**Initiative**: D6 + current Agility. Set once per conflict. Extreme Reflexes/Combat Veteran can modify.

**Range Categories**: Arm's Length → Near → Short → Long → Distant

**Armor Ratings**: Reduce damage taken. Scrap armor (AR3), Kevlar vest (AR6), vehicle hulls.

**Rot System**:
- Rot Points accumulated from Zone exposure, contaminated food/water, Rot Hotspots
- 10 Rot Points = permanent Rot Point (attribute permanently lost)
- Rot Level 0 (safe) / 1 (1 RP/day) / 2 (1 RP/hour) / 3 (hotspot only)
- Protection: Rot suit (PR3), Gas Mask (PR3), Diving Gear (PR3), Umbrella (PR3)
- Rot-Eater mutation neutralizes it

**Large-scale Ark Battle**: Opposing forces have Battle Levels (1/10 of Warfare DEV, rounded up). Each side rolls Base Dice equal to Battle Level. Results decide whether attackers breach/hold/are repelled. PCs influence outcome through individual heroics.

## SECTION 9: Zone Exploration

**The Zone Map**: Grid of sectors (~1 sq mile each). Mostly blank at campaign start. Filled in through Stalker expeditions. Explored sectors are generally safe on repeat visits.

**Creating a Sector** (GM rolls D66):
1. **Terrain type** — woods/marsh/ruins/industry/settlement
2. **Dominant ruin** (if any) — see table below
3. **Rot Level** (0-2, or 3 for hotspots)
4. **Threat Level** (1-4 fringe, 5-8 central, 9+ danger zone) → roll Base Dice equal to Threat Level: each / = 1 artifact, any = = there's a threat
5. **Threat type** — Humanoid (D66), Monster (D66), Phenomenon (D66)
6. **Artifact type** if present
7. **Stalker's Find the Path** roll
8. **Sector mood element** (optional flavor)

**Time to explore**: baseline is several hours per sector. Vehicles and some stunts halve this.

**Night travel**: Threat Level +3. Much more dangerous.

### Normal Ruins Table (D66)
Airplane Wreck, Amusement Park, Battlefield, Bus Station, Car Park, Church, Cinema, Crater, Dilapidated Mansion, Fast Food Joint, Gas Station, Highway, Hospital, Hunting Store, **Mall**, Marina, Museum, Office Building, Overgrown Park, Playground, Police Station, Radio Station, Residential Blocks, Road Tunnel, Ruined Bridge, School, Shelter, Skyscraper, Sports Center, Suburbia, Subway Station, Supermarket, Swimming Hall, Tank, Theater, Train Station

### Industrial Ruins Table (D66)
Factory, Firing Range, Military Base, Oil Tank, Pipeline, Power Line, Purification Plant, Radio Mast, Refinery, Rubbish Dump, Shipwreck, Shooting Range, Windmill

### Zone Phenomena (D66)
Acid Rain, Ash Storm, Dust Tornado, Electric Storm, Ghost Lights, Inertia Field, Magnetic Field, Mirage, Mud Puddles, Night Lights, Obelisk, Pillars of Light, Rot Hotspot, Rot Wind, Sinkhole, Temperature Drop/Heat Wave, Unexploded Ordnance, Vacuum, Zone Smog

### Sector Mood Elements (flavor, no mechanics required)
- **People**: Fresh corpse bound to pole, a written message on wall, tracks leading deeper, another Ark's patrol
- **Nature**: Lone surviving tree, contaminated creek, Zone flowers in strange colors, thick thistle undergrowth
- **Weather**: Torrential rain, thick fog, blazing sun, sudden blue sky, gathering storm, thunder
- **Remains**: Road sign with Old Age markings, rusted vehicle wreck (shelter?), torn movie poster, old checkpoint, D6 skeletons

## SECTION 10: Humanoid Factions & NPCs

### Zone Factions

**ZONE-GHOULS**
- Nomadic, nocturnal raiders. Move in large groups. Hoods/masks/plastic cloaks.
- Take 1 damage/hour in direct sunlight.
- STR3, AGI4, WIT3, EMP2; Move 3, Shoot 2, Fight 1; bicycle chain + slingshot
- Not entirely savage — the Pure Ones (Zone-Ghoul tribe at a water plant) are territorial but negotiable
- Their leader Lutrell: STR3, AGI3, WIT5, EMP3; Fight 2, Shoot 3, Manipulate 2; negotiation is possible

**HELLDRIVERS**
- Road warrior clan. Exiled mutants who found a working vehicle fleet (old car park).
- All mohawks, leather, spikes, skulls.
- STR4, AGI5, WIT2, EMP3; Shoot 3, Fight 2, Jury-Rig 1; scrap rifle + bicycle chain + scrap armor
- Led by **Ilona the Corpse-Eater** (powerful Corpse-Eater mutation).
- Bodyguard **Gaffa**: STR2, AGI4, WIT3, EMP3; Fight 3, Move 4, Shoot 4; Sprinter mutation
- **Raff** (thief, no loyalty): STR2, AGI4, WIT2, EMP2; Human Magnet mutation
- Vehicles: armored buggies, cars, motorcycles with flamethrowers/scrap cannons
- Base: multi-story car park with workshop, arena cage fights, slaves in basement
- Quest hook: rescue kidnapped Ark members being held for ransom/slaughter

**NOVA CULT**
- Psionic sect. Shaved heads and long robes. Underground missile silo hideout.
- STR2, AGI3, WIT5, EMP4; Move 2, Heal 1; mental mutations only (Mind Terror, Puppeteer, Pathokinesis, Pyrokinesis, Telepathy)
- 6 MP starting (vs normal 3). Powerful.
- Led by **Plutonia**: fanatical, believes a "luminescent child" will trigger apocalypse
- **Ohm** (pig farmer): rejected cult member with Reptilian mutation; may betray Plutonia
- **Abbetina** (young priestess): pregnant, defiant, could recruit PCs against Plutonia
- The silo contains an active missile. Launch triggers Zone-wide Rot contamination across 10 sectors.
- Quest hook: prevent launch, or exploit internal schism between Abbetina and Plutonia

**THE MECHIES / RUST CASTLE**
- Neutral trading post faction. Live in a beached ferry/shipwreck.
- Tiny in size, insanely agile and technically skilled.
- Run by **Nikkil** (Stalker): STR1, AGI5, WIT5, EMP3; Extreme Reflexes mutation; owns steamboat Steelcrest
- **Ylora** (Chronicler): STR1, AGI4, WIT5, EMP4; Insect Wings mutation; teaches and maps the Zone
- **The Whoppers**: 4 colossal mutant guards (7+ feet). Mutations: Four-Armed, Corpse-Eater, Acid Spit, Sonar
- **Rust Castle marketplace** = neutral ground. All factions can meet here. Weapons left at the door.
- The castle is slowly sinking. Secret saboteur (Roggi the Whopper) is working against the Mechies.
- Quest hook: help reinforce/rescue Rust Castle, participate in faction meetings

**THE CHAIRMAN'S ARK** (cannibal dictatorship)
- Zone-Ghoul leader adopted as a child by scientists; only one in the Ark who can father children.
- Males enslaved ("Givers") and eventually butchered for food.
- Operated like a cult, run on fear and enforced fertility.
- **The Chairman**: STR3, AGI4, WIT5, EMP5; Fight 2, Move 3, Shoot 4, Manipulate 5; Revolver artifact
- **Rebeth** (Enforcer): STR5, AGI4, WIT2, EMP3; Fight 4; Four-Armed + Extreme Reflexes (6 MP)
- **Krin** (Stalker doubter): STR3, AGI5, WIT4, EMP2; Flame Breath mutation; potential PC ally
- Three armored war-buses with flamethrowers. Battle Level 3 raiding force.
- Quest hook: discover the truth, rescue Givers, trigger revolt or bring down the Chairman

**BEAST MUTANTS**
- Intelligent upright animals (dog/wolf, rodent, monkey varieties). Escaped from a distant genetics lab.
- Not hostile by default, but wary. Primitive but functional language.
- Dog: STR4, AGI5, WIT3, EMP2. Rodent: STR2, AGI5, WIT5, EMP2. Monkey: STR3, AGI5, WIT4, EMP2.
- No mutations, no Mutation Points.
- Their kin still held by "the Watchers" in a mountain facility.

**CANNIBALS**
- White face paint. Very hard to reason with (Manipulate -3). Only show of force deters.
- STR5, AGI4, WIT3, EMP2; Fight 2, Move 1; 1 random mutation
- From an Ark where scientists died early, children raised themselves brutally

**DOOM CULTISTS**
- Worship a specific ruin/artifact. Try to recruit or sacrifice visitors.
- STR2, AGI3, WIT5, EMP4; mental mutations; may carry Eden clues

**WANDERERS**
- Traveling humans (non-mutants). From distant areas. Guard but not hostile. May have artifacts.

## SECTION 13: The Metaplot (Spoilers — GM Only)

**True Origin of the People**: Genetically engineered mutants. Created by Mimir enclave scientists as "Project Eden" — a program to engineer a non-aggressive successor race for humanity. Planted in Arks on Earth's surface as controlled experiments.

**The Apocalypse Sequence**:
1. Red Plague (contagious, bloodshot eyes → death within days)
2. Nuclear war → global collapse
3. Rich/powerful flee to **enclaves** (underground, ocean-floor, space)
4. Enclaves form power blocs called **Titan Powers**
5. **Enclave War** breaks out. Most enclaves destroyed or crippled.
6. Mimir enclave survives. Scientists relaunch Project Eden in secret.
7. Genetically engineered mutants planted in Arks across Earth's surface.
8. Scientists eventually abandon the experiment — flee to Arks, then die of Rot or despair.
9. The Elder (**Dr. Sebedius**) is the last surviving researcher. Has kept quiet for decades per protocol. His failing health is the campaign clock.

**What's at Eden**: The Project Eden command center. Hidden in the Zone. Contains the truth about the People, advanced technology, and ultimately a **one-way ticket to join humanity's new civilization in space**. What happens after reaching it is future-expansion material.

**Three artifact clues to Eden**:
1. Cassette Player — receives the Eden emergency broadcast
2. Video Camera — recorded footage of Eden / path to it
3. ID Card — physical access to the facility

**The Elder's role**: Can give cryptic clues but can't be forced to reveal everything. He's mentally diminishing. His death is a possible campaign catalyst.

**Usable for our game**: The "corporate conspiracy created the underclass" arc is direct translation. The Dawn Vault = information control. The Assembly = democratic resistance structure. The Elder = dying whistleblower figure.

## SECTION 14: Quest Hooks

### Hub-Focused

**The Drying Well**
Water source is contaminated or running low. Send expedition to find alternative source. Water Trader arrives at a price. Other Bosses hoard their private supplies. Who controls water controls the Ark.

**The Demagogue**
An Agitator Boss is giving speeches about rising up against the current order. People are listening. The established Bosses want the problem quietly handled. The Agitator may be right. Or may be a tool of a worse faction.

**The Faction Schism**
Two rival Bosses are on the edge of open war. The Fixer PC is approached by both sides. The Chronicler is writing the history. The Slaves are going to be the ones who suffer. Pick a side, negotiate peace, or profit from chaos.

**The Rogue Gearhead**
A Gearhead has built something dangerous and won't hand it to the Dawn Vault. A bomb. A vehicle. A device nobody understands. The Chroniclers want it. The Bosses want it. Someone sent a Stalker to steal it. The Gearhead is terrified.

**Ark Under Siege (Zone-Ghoul Threat)**
Zone-Ghouls are massing outside the walls. One arrived alone and asked to speak to the Elder. Nobody knows why. The Boss is mobilizing the Militia. The Elder's messenger wants to negotiate. Someone is going to start shooting before that happens.

### Expedition-Focused

**The Pure Plant** (Special Zone Sector, introductory)
An earlier expedition is missing. Water supply is running low. The Pure Plant ruins contain clean water — and Zone-Ghouls who built their society there. Negotiate with Lutrell, rescue survivors, recover the Diary artifact from the Trash Hawk's nest. Decision: share the water, drive off the Zone-Ghouls, or find another way.

**The Other Ark** (Special Zone Sector, mid-campaign)
Discovery of another settlement. Dark secret emerges. The Chairman fathers children — the only one who can — and runs a cannibal slave economy. The Stalker Krin doubts. The slave Dink might betray her. Armored war-buses could attack the PCs' Ark. Decision: expose/destroy the Chairman, recruit the dissidents, or keep quiet and leave.

**Stop the Doomsday** (Nova Cult Sector, high stakes)
The Nova Cult has an active missile and a fanatical leader about to launch. Abbetina (the pregnant priestess) needs help overthrowing Plutonia. The missile target, if launched, irradiates ten Zone sectors. Any of them could be home to another settlement. Decision: help Abbetina revolt, negotiate, or run.

**Rust Castle Intrigue** (Mechies sector, social/exploration)
Neutral marketplace, all factions present. The Chairman's representatives are here. So are the Helldrivers. And a Scrap Oracle who has been watching the PCs. The castle is sinking. One of the Whoppers (Roggi) is the saboteur. A faction is planning to take it by force.

**The Eden Trail** (campaign-long metaplot)
Three artifacts: Cassette Player, Video Camera, ID Card. Each leads closer to the Project Eden command center. Each is held by someone dangerous (or hidden in a deadly ruin). Finding all three and reaching Eden resolves the metaplot — the People learn the truth of their existence and can choose to leave Earth or stay.

## SECTION 15: Tone Notes & Vocabulary

### Tone Registers
MYZ is **quieter** than both IZ2 and JD. It's more folk-horror and community drama than action-first. The violence matters more because starvation and social collapse are the baseline threats. Compared to:
- **IZ2**: Less neon, more ash. Less transhumanism, more regression. Both have factions and hidden truths.
- **JD**: Less absurdist comedy, more genuine despair. JD uses satire; MYZ uses tragedy.

In our game: MYZ aesthetic = outer-ring/ruined sectors. IZ2/JD aesthetic = the hub and its factions.

### NPC Dialogue Flavor
- Everything is stated in terms of immediate survival needs: "I need grub," "that costs bullets," "the Rot will get us"
- Information is always transactional: knowledge = power = bullets
- References to Old Age objects with wonder and confusion: "a machine that shows moving pictures," "plastic curved tubes with glass inside — makes things look bigger"
- Temporal distance from the past: "the Ancients" are as mythological as ancient Romans
- No nostalgia for the past (unlike JD) — nobody alive remembers it

- **The Assembly** mechanic → faction vote events / world flag toggles
- **Boss types** → NPC faction leaders in hub areas (use the 9-type taxonomy)

- **Dawn Vault** → information-locked quest database; certain data requires Culture DEV equivalent

- **Rot Level** → sector-level hazard zone rating, maps directly to our hazard system
- **Threat Roll** → zone generation formula for procedural encounter spaces
- **Metaplot artifacts** → three-item questline structure for major story arc resolution
- **Session Body Count** → world flag that increments each session unless players intervene
- **Relationship to NPCs + Big Dreams** → drives EventDeckSystem personal quests
