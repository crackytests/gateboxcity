# Gatebox City — v0.0.7 Patch Notes

## New Content

### Ward 7 — Comfort Annexe (3-scene area)
A new multi-scene location added to the world: the Comfort Annexe, Gatebox Corporation's residential welfare installation. Three scenes:
- **Reception** — entry point; the paperwork is cheerful, the headcount is wrong
- **Ward Floor** — the pod grid shows more red than green; nursing terminal arms an emergent quest; one Pacification Warden and a failed graft from the crawlspace
- **Sublevel** — restricted level below the ward floor

Hub return after visiting Ward 7 now triggers consequence logic: Mister Static reacts to what you saw, a rescued resident settles into the squatters' unit, Big Gates sends a sweep team, and Linda issues a wellness check — all queued as event cards.

### Bone Dividend Vault
Boss arena accessible after the Ward 7 thread. Read General Bone Dividend's soul-accounting ledger, decide what to do with the racks of logged souls, and put the General down. Resolution surfaces back at the Big Gates Informant in the hub with branching outcomes based on whether you freed the souls.

## Enemy System Overhaul

### Telegraph Wind-Up System
Enemies with a `telegraph_ability` body part now wind up special attacks before executing them. During wind-up the enemy roots in place, and the triggering part's lock difficulty is eased so the player can target and shoot it to cancel the strike. A successful interrupt staggers the enemy (`stagger_on_interrupt` seconds of stun). Special attacks have their own cooldown separate from basic melee.

Special abilities dispatched by telegraph:
- `lunge` — closes distance at range with a damage multiplier
- `spray` — ranged slow/damage-over-time
- `grab` — restraint attack

### Pack Coordination
Enemies sharing a `pack_id` now coordinate:
- **Anchor buff**: while a `is_pack_anchor` enemy is alive, all pack members deal increased melee damage (`pack_buff_damage` multiplier)
- **Separation steering**: packmates apply a repulsion vector so they flank and spread rather than stack on a single tile
- **Aggro propagation**: an alerted pack member signals nearby allies within `pack_alert_radius`

### Detection Meter
Replaced instant aggro with a fill-over-time detection bar (0..1). Rate is modulated by faction (corporate units hesitate against heavily-modded targets; drift-hostile factions lock on faster) and by the player's stealth multiplier. Detection drains when line of sight breaks.

### Enrage System
Part-destruction consequences can now trigger an enrage: a temporary melee damage multiplier with a duration. Splice Arm enrage is now authored in `splice_splice_arm.tres` rather than hardcoded in `Splice.gd`.

### Data-Driven Part Consequences
`BodyPart` gains new exported fields read from `.tres` resources:
- `part_role` — classification hint for loot and UI
- `on_destroy_effect` — what happens when this part breaks (`slow`, `stun`, `enrage`, `volatile_explode`, `cloud`, `damage_reduction`, `defeat`, etc.)
- `effect_strength / effect_radius / effect_payload` — parameters for the effect
- `telegraph_ability` — the special attack this part arms
- `windup_lock_bonus` — how much easier this part becomes to lock during wind-up
- `volatile` — explodes on destroy
- `hackable` — can be taken over via hack terminal
- `weak_point` — reduced lock difficulty

`Splice.gd` and `RainMutant.gd` subclass logic stripped down; all destruction consequences now dispatch through the base `Enemy._on_part_destroyed` data handler.

### New Body Part Resources
- `splice_drag_frame.tres`, `splice_graft_shell.tres`, `splice_splice_arm.tres`, `splice_wire_skull.tres` — Splice gang cyborg parts
- `goon_head.tres`, `goon_torso.tres`, `goon_right_arm.tres`, `goon_left_leg.tres`, `goon_right_leg.tres` — Gatebox/Big Gates goon parts
- `fellar_amplifier_spine.tres`, `fellar_jaw_amplifier.tres`, `fellar_left_cable_whip.tres`, `fellar_right_cable_whip.tres`, `fellar_soul_resonator.tres`, `fellar_stage_core.tres` — Rocker Fellar boss parts

## GameState Expansion

### Player Attributes
IZ2-derived attribute matrix now stored in `GameState.attributes`:
- **MEAT**: STR, AGL, CON
- **MIND**: INT, PER, WIL
- **SOUL**: EMP, LUCK

Wired into 2d6 skill checks via the new `Dice.gd` system.

### Drift System
Estrangement from unmodified humanity. Rises on cybernetic installs. Flags at:
- 21+ `drift_noticed` — Gatebox grows attentive
- 41+ `drift_uncanny` — civilians uneasy
- 61+ `drift_scanned` — Preservation scans increase
- 81+ `high_drift` — unique NPC reactions and event cards

Drift-reaction ambient event cards now fire at appropriate thresholds.

### Soul-Rot System
Corruption of the soul anchor (0..100). Rises from freeing trapped souls, soul-tech use, and Preservation compliance. Full consequences coming in a later phase.

### Gatebox Attention Level
One-way-ish ratchet tracking corporate notice drawn by trespassing in Gatebox installations.

### Wan Notes (Currency)
`wan_notes` is now a dedicated `int` field on `GameState` rather than a counted inventory item. `add_wan_notes()` and `spend_wan_notes()` are the canonical API. The `Wan Note` inventory item remains as the display/legacy path.

### Consumables / Drugs
Four drugs authored in `GameState.CONSUMABLES` with active-buff and comedown-penalty phases:
- **Jolt** — speed up, reload faster; comedown drops aim accuracy
- **Glass** — aim bonus and hack bonus; comedown slows movement
- **Redline** — heavy damage buff; comedown increases damage taken
- **Patch** — instant heal (45 HP); short comedown movement slow

Each drug tracks `active_effects` entries with `phase: "active"|"comedown"` and remaining duration.

### Enemy Loot Tables
Faction-derived loot tables in `GameState.ENEMY_LOOT` keyed by `loot_id`. Implant drops are tied to body parts left **intact** — destroying a part forfeits its implant. Factions: `splice`, `gatebox`, `big_gates`. Scrap drop chances and special-item pools per faction.

`SCRAP_VALUES` dictionary for sell-only ruined parts. `IMPLANT_TIERS` for rarity classification (common/uncommon/rare).

### Dialogue System (Topic Conversations)
- `known_topics` — the player's keyword codex; builds as you talk
- `npc_disposition` — per-NPC attitude (0..100), seeded from faction on first encounter
- `conversation_tone` — "polite" / "normal" / "blunt", remembered between sessions

### Save Location
Player position, rotation, and health are now saved with the game and restored on load, dropping the player back in the exact scene spot rather than the level's default entrance.

## Interactive Event Cards

The `EventDeckSystem` now supports full choice-resolution cards with 2d6 attribute checks, branching outcomes, and faction consequences. New card types added to `WorldDirector`:

### Wan Moa Torai Thread
- **A Torai Collector** — a tithe demand; pay, talk it down (EMP check), or stare them down (WIL check with crit/fumble)
- **Collections, In Person** — two collectors blocking the corridor; pay, flee (AGL), or fight (STR)
- **A Torai Courtesy** — a goodwill envelope; take it or check it for tracking threads (PER)

### Gatebox Corporation Thread
- **Comfort Compliance Check** — wellness drone audit; comply, bury it in its own forms (INT), or walk past (AGL)
- **Preservation Detail** — two enforcers; pay the levy, break through (STR), or slip away (AGL)

### Drift-Reaction Ambient Cards
- **Something Off** (Marbles) — fires at `drift_noticed`, before `drift_uncanny`
- **Drift Advisory** (System X) — fires at `drift_uncanny`
- **Preservation Interest** (Gatebox Corporation) — fires at `high_drift`, persistent

### Ward 7 Fallout Cards
- **Big Gates Sweep** — armed after visiting the Comfort Annexe
- **Wellness Check** (Linda) — armed after visiting the Comfort Annexe

### New EventDeckSystem Features
- `pick_candidate()` — weighted draw without applying (for choice-resolution cards)
- `apply_effects()` — public API for branch resolution
- `expire_card()` — decrement/remove a card without triggering effects
- New effect types: `wan_notes` (cost/reward), `damage` (abstract injury), `add_drift`, `spawn_enemies` (live ambush), `remove_item`
- `_spawn_ambush()` — places a group of enemies ahead of the player in the live scene; skips in safe hubs

The level is rebuilt from scratch with five named zones:
1. **South Vestibule** — shelter, rain leak, entry corridor
2. **Atrium Void** — sludge gap, hanging escalators, elevator shaft, broad south ramp
3. **East Service Corridor** — back-of-house storage and ground flank route
4. **Relay Platform** — old mall broadcast deck, Store 4, debris piles
5. **West Maintenance Crawl** — low stealth shortcut to relay platform (replaces the hardlight bridge route)

**Added**: loot crate with salvage cache + ammo, `deliver`-type job node support (carry-and-drop instead of find-and-recover).
