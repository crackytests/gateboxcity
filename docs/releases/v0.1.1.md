# Gatebox City — v0.1.1 Patch Notes

A visual-polish and combat-tuning point release on top of v0.1.0.

---

## Hub NPCs Get Real Sprites

The Faded Atrium's residents now render as **8-direction billboard characters** instead of flat
props — Mister Static, Pipe Father Gideon, Vera, Kiki Baja, Ladderboy, Velvet Coil, and Brickmouth
Ronnie each have front / back / side / diagonal frames, per-character sizing, and face into the
atrium. (Vera's sprite set is new this release.)

---

## Rocker Fellar — Boss Visual Pass

- The General now shows **dedicated state art for his attacks** — distinct sprites for charge,
  sonic shockwave, and cable-whip wind-ups, so every telegraph reads at a glance.
- He flashes a **staggered sprite** when you blow off one of his body parts.
- Re-imported, higher-resolution boss art with corrected scaling and foot alignment.

---

## Combat & Enemy Tuning

- **Wall avoidance** — enemies now slide along and nudge around walls while chasing instead of
  grinding to a halt against corners and doorframes.
- **Tankier goons** — Gatebox goons gained body integrity and armor on the head, right arm, and
  torso, so they hold the line a bit longer.
- **Pack aggro tuning** — Splice duos use a tighter alert radius and lighter pack buff, so a single
  scout no longer chain-aggros an entire level; spawn profiles can set per-pack alert radius and
  buff values.

---

## Fixes & Polish

- **Directional billboard alignment** — sprites with an override texture (boss states, etc.) now
  align their feet to the ground correctly.
- **Leak Street dressing** — the patchwork door and window panels were rescaled and repositioned to
  sit properly in their frames.
- Added a Rocker Fellar single-sprite import tool and sprite-atlas debug helpers for art iteration.
