# Gatebox City — v0.1.0 Patch Notes

The biggest update yet. This release moves the game toward its original vision: enemies drop
cyberware, the whole economy runs on Wan Notes, jobs are dynamic and faction-sourced, an event-card
system follows you around with dice-rolled outcomes, NPCs hold real Daggerfall-style conversations,
and the campaign opens with Spooky Ghost waking up where he actually is — inside a borrowed android
body. The HUD has been rebuilt as an AR overlay, and combat got meaner.

---

## Who You Are Now — The Opening Retool

- **You wake in an android body.** Spooky Ghost cold-boots inside a salvaged android that Pipe
  Father Gideon had written off as dead, sheeted, and dumped in a bin. A scripted wakeup plays once
  on a new game.
- **The sheet ghost.** The burial sheet fused to the chassis when you woke it — you can't take it
  off, so you go through the lower city looking like a classic bedsheet ghost. NPCs notice. They ask
  about the sheet. They are not always kind about it. (It's a running gag; occasional asides surface
  on later conversations.)
- **The System X terminal is dead until you touch it.** First contact boots **Face's failsafe**,
  which recognizes your soul-signature and explains, cryptically, what you are — before handing the
  channel to **System X (Yoko and Pee Kid)**, who crossed time to help you and are openly exasperated
  that you walked off the mission to save the future.
- **NPCs are startled on first meeting** — a strange android (in a sheet) walking up and talking
  gets a reaction in-character from everyone in the mall, Cooters, and Suitors.

---

## Cyberware Loot & The Wan Note Economy

The old exchange/credit system is gone. Everything now runs on **Wan Notes**.

- **Enemies drop cyberware.** On death there's a chance to drop an implant, weighted toward body
  parts you left **intact** — break a part and you forfeit its implant. Rarer implants drop less
  often.
- **Velvet Coil installs them** for Wan Notes (each install adds drift, by rarity tier).
- **Pipe Father Gideon buys** unwanted implants and **ruined implant parts** for scrap value.
- **Ruined parts** (Cracked Optic, Bent Actuator, Fried Cortex Chip, Leaking Cell) are sell-only
  scrap for now.
- Shopkeepers and pharmacy prices re-tuned to Wan Notes throughout.

---

## 2d6 Stat Checks

A new dice core underpins event resolutions and dialogue checks: **2d6 + attribute modifier +
relevant cyberware/drug bonus vs. a difficulty.** Double-sixes auto-succeed (crit), double-ones
auto-fail (fumble). Difficulty bands at 6 / 8 / 10 / 12. Eighteen implants map to specific check
bonuses (force, flee, analyze, read, resist, sneak, etc.).

---

## Dynamic Cooters Jobs

- **The job board shuffles every time you enter Cooters.** Authored job templates roll a concrete
  instance each time: a destination, a threat band, a contesting rival faction, and a Wan Note
  reward (with an occasional bonus item).
- **Three objective types:** recover a marked item (*find*), bring back specific enemy-drop loot
  (*kill-for-loot*), or carry a parcel to a drop-off (*deliver*).
- Jobs are sourced from factions/NPCs, pay in Wan Notes, and move faction reputation.

---

## Enemy-Layout Profiles & a New Android

- **Quest locations now field data-driven, faction-themed garrisons** that scale with the job's
  threat band — Splice, Gatebox, or Big Gates contest each site with their own enemy mix. Bosses and
  minibosses are excluded from these shuffled spawns.
- **New enemy — Gatebox Sentinel.** A fast, fragile corporate android (a cousin of the shell you
  woke up in): closes distance quickly, strikes fast, folds under sustained fire. Joins the Gatebox
  garrisons and is the signature foe of the dream.
- **Garrison spawn distances fixed** so the first enemies no longer aggro the instant you arrive —
  you get to advance and pick the engagement.

---

## Event Cards & Faction Threads

- **Interactive event cards** now fire on travel, on leaving Cooters, and on returning to the hub.
  Cards present narrative choices, many resolved by a **2d6 stat check** with success / failure /
  crit / fumble branches, and can chain into further cards.
- **Faction event chains** auto-link to your active job — the giver may demand a cut on the way out,
  the rival faction may ambush you en route, and a grateful follow-up can surface back at the atrium.
- **Hostile event outcomes spawn real enemies.** A failed escape or fight in an ambush now drops a
  faction-appropriate enemy group on you (plus an opening hit), instead of an abstract damage number.

---

## Daggerfall-Style Dialogue System

NPC conversations are now a full topic system:

- **Category tabs** (Tell me about / Where is / Work / Any news? / Services / Goodbye), a **keyword
  codex** that grows by word of mouth, a **tone selector** (Polite / Normal / Blunt), and per-NPC
  **disposition** seeded from faction standing and nudged by tone.
- **Rumors** ("Any news?") pull world-state-aware lines, and topics teach new keywords.
- **All non-spine NPCs migrated** to authored profiles. **Marbles' job board** runs through the
  conversation now (a Services option, with payout collected as a Work topic).
- **Statement mode** gives objects, consoles, and one-shot story beats the same framed window
  without forcing topic trees onto inanimate things.

---

## Rep-Gated Campaign Threads

- **Wake-Up Call is now a dream** behind a door that appears in the mall once System X is online
  (it left the travel gate).
- **Rocker Fellar** is now a **System X thread** — System X only hands you the lead once you've
  earned their trust (rep gate).
- **The Comfort Annexe (Ward 7)** now requires **Gatebox standing** — you have to have played their
  game (complied at checkpoints, paid their levies) before the ward reads you as a Comfort Citizen.
- More jobs and events give and take faction reputation to make those gates reachable and meaningful.

---

## HUD Redesign — Android AR Overlay

The HUD is reconceived as the AR display Spooky Ghost sees through his android optics: clean techno
with subtle occult accents, a dual cyan + magenta palette, built from framed "widget" panels.

- **Removed the redundant always-on readouts** (inventory, reputation, cybernetics, sky/generator
  status) — that data lives in the GhostTerm and menus now.
- **Directional damage indicators** — every hit shows an edge arrow on the reticle ring pointing at
  its source (rotated to your facing), alongside the damage number. Multiple attackers each get an
  arrow; environmental damage just shows the number.
- **Auto-fading log** — the log flashes on a new line, holds, then settles to a faint resting state.
  It uses a monospace, slightly-italic "console" font so it reads distinctly from spoken dialogue.
- **AR framing** — cyan widget frames, a faint full-screen visor border, and a magenta soul-anchor
  sigil accent.

## Combat Improvements

- **Enemies turn hostile the instant you attack them** — no line of sight required — and wake nearby
  allies.
- **Splice lunges forward for real** (its frame-lunge now physically closes the distance) and can
  **leap over obstacles** to reach you.
- **Goon "trash cannon" goes long-range** — goons now open fire from across a room while they
  advance.

---

## Effects & Audio

- **VCR-rewind load transition** — loading the game now plays a tracking-scramble effect timed to the
  existing rewind sound.

---

## Fixes & Polish

- **Deliver jobs** no longer mint a duplicate parcel at the drop-off; the carried parcel is handed
  over and consumed on payout.
- **Retired the legacy hub cyberware kiosk** (item-cost self-install) — cyberware installation is
  unified on Velvet Coil and Wan Notes.
- **System X implies it knows your history with Linda** without spelling it out.
- The **escalator console** speaks as System X (Ladderboy isn't in the hub yet).
- The **rain-mutant containment** prompt now says to drag it "near the door" instead of a magenta pad.
- Economy balance pass across drop rates, install/scrap values, and check difficulties.

---

## Removed

- The mission-board, status-archive, and cyberware-kiosk props in the Faded Atrium hub.
- The old exchange/credit currency system (superseded by Wan Notes).
