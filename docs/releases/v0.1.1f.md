# Gatebox City — v0.1.1f Patch Notes

A small hotfix on top of v0.1.1.

---

## Job Destination Visual Fix

The interactive markers and exits in the four Cooters job destinations (Pipe Utility Tunnels, Dead
Food Court Bloom, Water Reclamation Cistern, Collapsed Service Atrium) were rendering as blown-out
solid-color blobs that read like broken placeholder textures.

- **Interactable nodes** (valves, the Saint Ratchet relic, sample bulbs, the generator coupling,
  etc.) are now **glowing beacons** — a small core orb inside a thin halo ring lit by a soft point
  light, with toned-down emission so they no longer bloom into flat discs. Each node keeps its
  identifying color.
- **Exit doorways** are now a **textured metal hatch with a lit trim frame** instead of a flat
  solid-color panel.

Gameplay is unchanged — same interaction zones, IDs, and prompts; only the visuals were rebuilt.

---

## Other Fixes

- **Returning from Cooters** now drops you at the Cooters door in the Sub-Sub-Basement district
  instead of the default spawn.
- **Main menu music** added.
- Further **Leak Street prop dressing** alignment fixes (vents, barrels, crates, market props).
