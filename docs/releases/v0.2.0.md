# Gatebox City — v0.2.0 Patch Notes

A big content drop: a full **Quest & Event Depth** sprint deepens the job loop, and a large new
location — **The Gantry Tier** — connects Leak Street, the Comfort Annexe, and Rocker Fellar Keep.

---

## New Location: The Gantry Tier

A large, walkable transit interchange under the artificial sky — neutral ground between the
districts, and now the connective tissue of the lower city.

- **It connects everything.** Leak Street's travel gate now routes to the Gantry Tier instead of
  jumping you straight to the Keep or the Annexe. From the Tier you physically walk to:
  - **Leak Street** (open),
  - the **Comfort Annexe** portal (still Gatebox-rep-gated, at the door),
  - the **Rocker Fellar Keep** service lift (still quest-gated).
  Leaving the Annexe or the Keep now drops you back onto the Tier at the right door.
- **Under the false sky.** The toxic-rain cycle runs overhead just like Leak Street, with shelters
  (and a guaranteed refuge in the undercroft) for counterplay. No garrison — it's shared territory.
- **Verticality.** A walkable ramp descends from the concourse into an enclosed lower **undercroft**
  — a maintenance level with its own lighting, a rain refuge, support structure, and a one-time
  **Maintenance Cache** (a Gatebox Eye implant + Wan). Fall off the deck and you land here
  recoverably instead of in a void.
- **Independent squatters camp.** A Scavenger Boss (with a **salvage sell-post**), a Comfort Annexe
  **Escapee** (one-time intel for System X rep), and a Tier Drifter — plus a readable dead
  **departure board**, a stripped derelict tram, and scattered freight.

### Gatebox patrol (reads your Gatebox standing)
- **Neutral** (decent rep): two sentinels **walk a patrol beat** and a **Patrol Officer** runs a
  comfort audit you can submit to (comply for standing, or talk your way out) — once per visit.
- **Provoke them** (shoot a sentinel) and the truce collapses: the officer joins the fight, the
  whole patrol turns hostile, and your Gatebox standing takes a hit.
- **Hostile** (you've already crossed Gatebox): three sentinels hunt you on arrival.
- All paths drop Gatebox salvage and log to the **Gatebox Sentinel** compendium entry.

## Quest & Event Depth (Sprint B)

### Day cycle + rest
- Jobs no longer reshuffle every time you enter Cooters. The board now refreshes **once per day**.
- A **rest cot** in the Faded Atrium lets Spooky bed down: it advances the day, repairs the chassis
  to full, and posts a fresh job board. The day count persists across saves.

### Reward variety
- Jobs can now pay out **cyberware implants** and **world-flag unlocks** on top of Wan Notes, items,
  and faction rep. Reward implants drop as carried implants you install at the Velvet Coil.

### More jobs + the escort objective
- Six new job templates across new givers, factions, threat bands, and the find / kill-loot /
  deliver objective types.
- A brand-new **escort** objective: find a stranded asset deep in a destination and walk it back to
  the exit alive. It follows you, takes fire if enemies crowd it (protect it by positioning), and
  shows a live status callout. Lose it and you can re-run the job. Three escort templates added.

### Branchier faction events
- Six new faction event cards woven into the Splice / Gatebox / Big Gates / Torai threads, with
  follow-on chains, two-way reputation swings, and item/implant payoffs — including a safehouse
  cache that pays off an earlier System X job.

## Other
- New `damaged` signal on enemies (lets allies/observers react to a hit without changing the hit
  enemy's own behavior) and a `patrol_neutral` mode (walks a beat without auto-aggro, still fights
  back if attacked).
