# Interface Zero 2.0 — White Room Reference
*Extracted from the core rulebook for use in our game. Organized by usefulness.*

---

## 1. SETTING TONE & WORLD

**Year:** 2090. The world has balkanized into dozens of city-states, nations, and corporate enclaves.

**Key recent events:**
- **The Flare** (2088): A solar flare (possibly caused by Charon AI) knocked out the Global DataNet's GENIE filters for ~1 week. ~750 million casualties from resulting riots and violence. People's TAPs went dark. This is the origin of the TAP "off switch" hot patch.
- **Charon**: A rogue AI that emerged around 2089. Crashed the Eurasian Union central bank, wiped $45 trillion. Suspected to have triggered the Flare. First AI to achieve true self-awareness at scale. Still at large inside the Global DataNet.
- **Second American Civil War**: Left Chicago nuked (3 dirty nukes). It recovered as the "Free City of Chicago."
- **Chinese expansion into Russia**: China occupies everything east of the Urals. Russian insurgency ongoing.
- **GENIE**: The satellite/mesosphere/cloud wireless network that blankets the entire Earth. Powers all TAP connectivity. Developed in phases 2013–2023.

**The vibe (Billy Black Eyes speaking):**
> *"Protect and serve? What a load of crap. The streets are a war zone, and if you wear the badge, you're marked for death omae. Get strapped. The only person you can count on in the concrete jungle is you."*

**Class structure:**
- **Gargoyles (Goyles)**: Penthouse elites, untouchable, enormous security
- **Uprights**: Upper middle management, corp types, rarely in the sprawl
- **Crats (Corpotrash)**: Lower-floor tower workers, stock options, slumming for thrills — good customers
- **Bloopers**: Average people just trying to survive
- **Bangers/Sprawlers**: Street-level. Everyone knows there are no old gangers.

**Prison:** Virtual reality prisons. Bodies in coffin tanks. 1 year VR = 1 day real time. "Mind-stretch." Serving a 7-year sentence takes only a week of real time.

## 2. TECHNOLOGY

### The TAP (Tendril Access Processor)
The TAP is the universal neural interface implanted in virtually everyone. Think: brain-native smartphone fused with augmented reality overlay. Accessed via thought.

**What it does:**
- Connects you to the Global DataNet (GodNet / The Deep) at all times
- Streams music, MediaProgs (TV), VR domains, hyper reality overlays
- Social media (MyFace), news, communication
- For hackers: the primary attack/defense tool
- Renders **Hyper Objects** — AR overlays visible only to TAP users. Gangs use hypertags instead of spray paint. Corps project ads. Games overlay combat VR on the real world.
- Can be turned OFF via a 30-second shutdown sequence (since the Flare patch)

**TAP addiction:** Clinics exist for GodNet addiction. Heavy users stop distinguishing reality from The Deep. Mental Hindrances can come from TAP overuse/abuse.

**Meme infection:** A hacked advertisement with no safety filters can flood your consciousness with billions of memes and burn out your personality in seconds. (NPC encounter type: burned-out meme addict.)

**Firewall:** Each person's TAP has a Firewall rating — their defense against hacking.

### The Global DataNet / The Deep / GodNet
These terms are interchangeable in common use. It's the internet but native to your brain. Everywhere, always on. Carried by the GENIE satellite/cloud network.

**The Deep** is the colloquial term (grungy). **GodNet** is what conspiracy types call it (it IS basically god — it tells people where to go, what to think, what to buy). **Global DataNet** is the formal term.

**Hyper Reality (HR):** Full sensory VR overlay on the real world. The "Chicagowars" example: a 14-year-old using a VR filter that turns a pen into a blaster and strangers into fantasy monsters she hunts for points. The real world is there underneath, but HR makes it a game.

**HR abuse:** Exec who became so addicted to HR she now believes she is an elven queen. Cannot distinguish reality.

**The Deep as prison:** VR prisons run at 365:1 time ratio.

### Cyberware / Augments
**Grades of augments (quality tiers):**
- **Gutterware**: Black market, cheap, rough. Highest Strain cost. The chrome reapers' stock.
- **Streetware**: Standard. Available from fixers and semi-legit clinics.
- **Milware**: Military-grade. Never on open or black market. Requires megacorp-level connections to obtain.
- **Hyperchrome**: Top-of-the-line consumer grade.

**Strain**: The psychological/biological cost of augmentation. Cybernetics have a Strain cost; exceed your limit and you lose your mind.

**Useful augment ideas for our NPCs / found items:**
- Cybereyes (night vision, enhanced vision, infra/ultra optics, flash compensators)
- Cyberears (amplified hearing)
- Bone Reinforcement I-III (+1 Toughness each)
- Subdermal Armor / Advanced Subdermal Armor
- Muscle Augmentation (Strength up)
- Enhanced Neural Net (Smarts up)
- Enhanced Articulation (Agility up)
- Cyberweapon (retractable claws/blades, always armed)
- Tactical Computer (draws extra initiative card)
- Trauma Suppressor (ignore 1 wound penalty — but overexertion worsens wounds)
- Drug Reservoir (implanted gland produces up to 3 doses of a combat drug)
- Data Carrier (implanted encrypted hard drive)
- Tailored Pheromones (+2 Charisma with organic beings)
- Gecko Hands (Wall Walker ability)
- Skill Computer / Knowcomp (grants a skill at d6/d8)
- Vehicle Control Interface I-III (neural drone/vehicle control)
- Wireless Reflexes I-III (+1 Parry per level, -1 to ranged attacks against you)
- Cyberskull Protean (face shifts every 4 hours, -2 to recognize you)
- Cyberskull Enforcer (+2 Intimidation, +1 head armor, looks thuggish)

**Bioware** (biological augments): Beautification Treatment (+Charisma), Biofeedback (Spirit up), Anti-Toxin System, Cybergills (breathe underwater), Cyberlungs (air tank implanted), Environmental Regulators.

**Chrome reapers**: Street criminals who mug augmented people and strip their cyberware to resell. They study targets to identify valuable implants before attacking. Magnetic clamps, flashburst grenades to disable power cells.

### Dubbing (Consciousness Backup)
For 250,000 credits, you can have your consciousness backed up to a cortex drive or remote engram bank. If you die, you're downloaded into a new vat-grown body. Immortality for the rich. Does not include cyberware — you must re-purchase all implants.

**Engrams:** Downloadable skill chips. Loaded into Active Memory Sectors (AMS) of your TAP. You can run skills you don't have for the duration of a scene. Illegal AI Sprites can be coded as engrams — this is how Charon was born.

### Drones & Golemmechs
- **Drones**: Remotely piloted via TAP/VCI. Basic mode = remote commands. Pilot mode = consciousness transferred to drone, -4 penalty to meat body.
- **Golemmechs**: Bipedal mechs, used by all major militaries. Light (recon, urban), Medium (frontline), Heavy (fortress defense). Ravenlocke fields them. Common in the streets of major cities.
- **Cyber Hounds**: Cybernetically enhanced dogs. Subdermal armor, go-for-throat instinct. Traded in dog-fighting rings or used as gang status symbols/guardians.

### Drugs (Street/Combat)
Betameth patches (keep you alive/functioning while badly wounded). Designer drugs from South America. "Grace" (mentioned as highly addictive, used in the vice economy). Stim-sticks (caffeine-analogue). Psychotropic malware (hacked via TAP to induce states — used in interrogation).

### Golemmech / VR Domain
Corporate entertainment: Full sensory VR domains. "Simex" — direct cortex stimulation at pleasure dens like Anastasia's. Serenity Tower's "sensory immersion chambers."

## 3. FACTIONS & ORGANIZATIONS

### Megacorps

**RAVENLOCKE SECURITIES**
*"Effective Law Enforcement at Competitive Prices."*
The dominant military/security corp. Has HQs in every major city. Provides guards, golemmechs, police contracting. Runs prisons ("rehabilitation centers"). Has black ops teams, suspected zeek-powered squads. Runs North American Coalition (NAC) enforcement in Chicago. Their armor brand covers everything from riot gear to spec ops suits. Parent company to Nortec. Ubiquitous, ruthless, basically the face of law enforcement to street level people.
- **Our game use**: The "corp security" presence. Branded patrol units, armory signage, the logo on the bad guys. Cooters jobs often involve going somewhere Ravenlocke doesn't want you.

**NORTEC (a Ravenlocke holding)**
Gene-splicing and simulacrum manufacturing. "Pro-zeek" public face. Scanners zeek minds of newly vat-born sims. Hoffman Estates LiveFac in Chicago is their simulacrum factory. Nortec Tower in Chicago (4,367 feet, world's tallest — lower levels go equally deep underground).
- **Our game use**: The "corporation that made things" vibe. Simulacra NPCs could be Nortec products.

**CHIMERA**
Pioneer of simulacra engineering. Created the genetic-manipulation foundations for all artificial life. Arcology in Chicago for employee protection. Controls Jupiter's moons in the solar colonies. Rumored to have alien DNA.
- **Our game use**: The source of exotic biohorrors, escaped clones, illegal hybrid experiments.

**THIRD LIFE INNOVATIONS (TLI)**
Gene-splicing, DNA recombination, "reestablished species." Controversial and anti-ethics. Arcologies near every major center of operations. Research facilities in Chicago and Johannesburg. Rumored to work with alien DNA from Mars.
- **Our game use**: The body horror / mad science faction. TLI is where experiments come from.

**GOLDEN PROMISE, INC.**
Heavy tech industries: android and military development. Insular, secretive. Runs Golden City on Mars. Ruthless with internal staff.

**GALAXY ENTERTAINMENT**
Media megacorp. Full-sensory VR domains, simsex, MediaProgs. Chicagoland HQ contains production studios AND consumer-grade sensory immersion chambers. Accused of using zeeks to embed subliminal messaging in broadcasts.
- **Our game use**: The entertainment/media flavor. Their presence explains VR arcade pods, simex dens.

**EMPEROR PHARMACEUTICALS**
Present in Boston. One of their arcologies was targeted in the terrorist attacks that opened the Charon era. Big pharma in a world where you can't afford a real doctor.

**MALBRAND CORP**
Chicago-based mass-market everything corp. "If it's up for sale, it probably has a Malbrand logo." Their corp tower is bigger than Nortec Tower's footprint, functions as a mega-mart. Regular workers go there for cheap entertainment.
- **Our game use**: This is basically the spiritual ancestor of our mall. A mall that IS a corporate tower.

**JATA TECH**
Africa-based. HQ in Timbuktu. Genetics and biotech research. Feeds discoveries to global corps.

**THE COUNCIL**: Anonymous governing body. Masks their faces with virtual constructs on HR channels. Have military influence to enforce orders. Deeply suspicious — no one knows if they're human, hybrid, or AI. Present in every sector.

**CHICAGO DEFENSE FORCE (CDF)**: The NAC's theocratic secret police in Chicago. The "Congregational Doctrine of Faith." Priests-as-police. Anti-simulacrum, anti-zeek extremists. Believed behind industrial bombings and sabotage against CHIMERA, Nortec, TLI.

**THE WOLVES**: The dominant gang/syndicate power in Chicago. Referenced throughout but not fully detailed — they're the big fish in the Chicago power stew.

**THE BURNERS**: Hybrid and mutant gang. Occupy the Arlington Heights rad zone. "Welcome all comers who dare to defy that claim." Nature/totem themed.

**AURORA ANGELS OF DEATH**: Control the Aurora Barrens near The Wall. Blood-soaked fists. Funded by smuggling goods and people into the city.

**LOKI'S LIARS**: Present in Arlington Heights. Gang with psychotropic drug connections.

**MUERTO 13**: Referenced in passing as having "squashed the Southies" in a block war.

**SEOULPA RINGS**: Korean Town gang presence. Ignore you if you don't bother their business.

**THE NATION OF PSIONS**: Psion supremacist group/cult. Arrogant, eugenic, despised even by other gangs. Control the Western Wing of Mersmeyer Tower.

**THE FREE FLOW RAIDERS**: One of 5 gangs sharing the Mersmeyer Tower under Dr. Meppen's "peace." Currently leaderless after "surgical complications."

## 4. STREET SOCIAL HIERARCHY & ROLES

These are the IZ2.0 archetypes — map directly to our NPC types:

### Who You Meet in the Sprawl

**Artists**: Forgers. Fake IDs, dummy credit wafers. Freelancers, live by their rep. One bad job ruins them. Charge a lot because mistakes get you killed.

**Bookmakers**: Run odds on anything. Fix the point spread. Work for mobs or freelance.

**Breakers**: Physical thieves. Burglary, car jacking, safe cracking. Don't hurt people directly — prefer clean theft.

**Crackers**: Black-hat hackers. Bust open systems, run wild in the DataNet. Counter-crackers. Every gang needs some.

**Fabbers**: Use nanomachines to fabricate illegal goods — everything from copyright-cracked items to bioweapons. Keep nano machines hidden with suicide code.

**Fixers**: The Mr. Johnsons. Acquisition specialists, dealers, fences, pimps, contractors. They have a thing you need, for a price.

**Street Docs / Cutters**: Unlicensed doctors. Operate out of back rooms, apartments. Charge 50% up front. Can get you feeling-goods and Betameth patches. Usually in debt to or protected by a gang.

**Patch Man**: Field medic variant. Nanobots and chem patches for combat situations.

**Ronin**: Deniable assets. Urban warriors. Raiders, smuggling ops, data snatches, milk runs through gang turf. Code of Honor. Follow their own rules.

**Grifters**: Social engineers. Con men. Boardroom or barroom, it doesn't matter. Oldest profession (no, the other one).

**Media Icons / Investigative Journalists**: Underground reporters. Expose corp/gov corruption via the Deep. Most news is fake; these guys risk their lives for real stories.

**Escort**: "It's a job." High-end arm candy for corp events, or straight-up sex work in the gutter. Gets gifts from clients hoping for "more."

**Infobrokers**: News hounds. Buy and sell information. Risk everything for the real story.

**Street Cleaners**: "Garbage men" who go into the seediest parts of the sprawl to erase illegal hypertags, deal with bodies. Plague control evolved into general problem removal. High mortality rate.

**Jump Bike Racers**: Loop through corporate downtown, wasteland rally courses, gang turfs. "One night I'm screaming through the Loop, the next I'm dodging bullets."

**Sector Cops**: Badge makes them targets. Either dirty (bribeable, predictable) or honest (worse for everyone, because they actually enforce the law). Outnumbered everywhere.

## 5. RACES / CHARACTER TYPES

These give us NPC visual variety and social tension hooks:

**Android (Andies, Machine, Scab, Trashcan)**: Born on assembly lines. Plastic/polymer bodies, fiber-optic wiring. Nano-brain. "A waffle iron probably has more rights than you do." Had an existential epiphany and left their corp. Rights: almost none.

**Bioroid**: Organic android. Matrix barcode tattooed on skin (shoulder/back of neck) if legal. Illegal unmarked bioroids can be executed on sight. "Vat baby." Many countries have outright banned them. Japan prefers them over simulacra.

**Cyborg (Cybershells, Tin Man)**: Human brain in android body. Military or megacorp sponsored, mostly. More and more going private sector. Full weapons loadout. Made the decision to sacrifice feeling to survive failing health.

**Human 2.0**: Genetically designed humans. Gene-tweaked for "perfection." "There's nothing 'neo' about us — the concept of Human 2.0 is degrading to our species." Protest subject. Stronger immune system, faster response, rapid healing — but encoded to perform specific functions. Beautiful People trait.

**Hybrid (Moreau, Gene Junkie, Frankenstein)**: Gene therapy spliced with animal DNA. Could be for fashion or military purposes. Sometimes the animal instincts are too strong. Aquatic hybrids, Fierce hybrids (tiger/wolf/honey badger), Primate hybrids (like King Louie, the "walking, talking orangutan" crime lord of Mogadishu). Some communities form gangs based on their animal type (the Burners).

**Simulacra (Sim, Simmie, Vat Baby, Replicant)**: Vat-grown biological constructs with implanted memories of a life that never happened. "More human than human." Different models:
- **Laborer**: Built for hazardous/menial work. Durability. 10-year designed lifespan. Batches look alike.
- **Luxury**: "Anything from kid-sized stuffed animals to entertainers to advanced prostitution models." +2 Charisma.
- Illegal sims sold at black markets like Chicagoland Stadium.

**Zeek (Psion)**: Humans with psychic abilities, triggered/amplified by TAP neural interface technology. Telekinesis, pyrokinesis, mind reading, remote influence. Hated and feared by mainstream society. Government wants to use them. Corporate black ops teams use them. TAP technology has caused a spike in confirmed zeeks since its release. "I don't believe in coincidence." UN recognized them as a minority ethnic group.

## 6. LOCATIONS (useful for level design)

### Mall-Relevant / Hub-Relevant Locations from Chicago

**YORKTOWN SHOPPING CENTER**: "Two million square feet of all the cheap clothes, food, and VR domains you can buy. You could spend days in the center and not see everything it has for sale. From the cyber to the virtual to the real, you'll find any amenity you need there, and then some." ← **This is a direct spiritual ancestor of our MallHub.**

**MALBRAND CORP TOWER**: Functions as both corporate HQ AND a mega-mart so large you need automated carts to traverse it in a day. People use it for cheap entertainment. This is "a mall that is also a corporation."

**SERENITY TOWER**: 48 stories of luxury lounges, bars, massage parlors, spas, jewelry stores — never closes. Corporate clients softened for sales pitches. Requires a Titanium Membership. No one leaves without spending 1,000 credits.

**CHICAGOLAND STADIUM**: The biggest ongoing black market in the sprawl. Illegal sims, virtual domains, drugs, glitched weapons, sector cops in civvies browsing. Overseen by Merc Matron who takes a cut from all vendors and funds private security.

**ANASTASIA'S**: Underground club. Choose any avatar you want — no real faces shown. Deep-addicted junkies get cerebral cortexes directly triggered. "A few seconds into an eternity of white-hot ecstasy." Without TAP active: people lying in cyber-pods staring blankly into space.

**CHOPPERS**: Famous fighting pit. Nightly bouts ending in maiming or death. Betting runs before first deaths. "Takes a few years of fighting in minor clubs to get a chance at the ring."

**THE CULT**: Faux-religious nightclub that mocks religions of the week. Employees dressed as monks, nuns, gurus. Clients encouraged to indulge in "sin." Real priest on premises for absolution (for a fee).

**SANCTUARY CIGARS**: Ex-merc owned cigar bar. Back room: military-grade network, database of deniable assets and work, black market auction house for rare weapons. Also sells literal sanctuary — a secure 3-meter cube where you can lie low. Neutral zone.

**FOX LAKE BIODOME**: Built over a former recreational lake that now houses a water processing plant. Several thousand residents live inside. Multi-year waiting list for entry. Better living conditions than anywhere else in the city.

**ANTIOCH**: "Self-named religious subsector." Every world religion has space. Temples, mosques, churches, all within walking distance. Tension but general tolerance. Largest congregation: Center for Universal Harmony.

**LUNATIC FRINGE**: Nightclub at the edge of a rad zone, between two warring gang territories (Burners + Loki's Liars). Ground floor: bars and pleasure dens. Psychotropics mixed into cocktails. The two gangs keep peace on the premises. Bouncers: Borghild (cybernetic senses, sweeps for malcontents) and Neal (bull hybrid, rides a tiny pink scooter to work to lure idiots into attacking him).

**MERSMEYER TOWER**: Corporate shell taken over by 5 gangs under enforced peace by Dr. Meppen (street doc with leverage over all leaders). Lowest levels: commercial area, stores frequented by different gangs, boycotted by others. Top floor: Dr. Meppen's medical suite. Ravenlocke watches it for signs of new syndicate formation. Off-the-books pharmaceutical testing finances it all.

**THE CANDY FACTORY (Sunny Heights Condoplex)**: 100 stories of the cheapest housing jammed with the least upstanding citizens. Gang rule by floor. "Anything and everything can be bought and sold, including your sanity and soul."

**GARY HELL ZONE**: The worst sector. Bombed-out, gang-infested wasteland. No security force will enter. Bodies pile up in streets. Entering alone = instant high-risk.

**THE MAZE**: Snarl of streets popular for jump bike racing, road wars, illegal motorsport. Far from Ravenlocke patrols. Place your bets before the deaths start.

**THE LOOP (Chicago downtown)**: "The Ant Farm." Every major Chicago business crammed into 2 square miles. Hackers and fixers everywhere. Nortec Tower is here.

## 12. SPECIFIC HOOKS FOR OUR GAME

### Direct Mall Hub Inspirations
- **Malbrand Corp Tower**: A megacorp that IS a mall. People use it for entertainment. Our mall as corporate property being contested/reclaimed fits this exactly.
- **Yorktown Shopping Center**: "Two million square feet of cheap clothes, food, and VR domains." The explicit cyberpunk mall. We're building this.
- **Mersmeyer Tower**: An abandoned corporate shell taken over by factions, each controlling a wing/floor — the boss of each wing is a street doc with leverage over all. **This is the exact template for a multi-faction hub building.**

### Factions for Hub Expansion (IZ2.0-inspired, adapt for our game)
- A Ravenlocke outpost (wanting to establish "legitimate" security presence — has its own agenda)
- A street doc running a clinic (neutral ground, leverage over everyone)
- A Galaxy Entertainment node (VR pod arcade, media, information)
- A Nortec employee/defector (access to simulacra, gene tech)
- A fixer's "office" (the jobboard itself — Mr. Johnson works out of the mall)
- A black marketeer in the basement (buys stolen Cooters cargo, sells to the underground)

### Dialogue Style (NPC voice templates)
IZ2.0 uses an in-world internet forum format. Every setting section is a #INFODUMP posted by named users, with comment threads below. The style is:
- Blogger voice: casual, first-person, opinionated
- Comment threads contradict or expand the main post
- Every NPC has a "hashtag" handle (#Billy_Black_Eyes, #Simba, #Magpie)
- Tone: cynical, street-smart, dark humor, genuine care buried under posturing

This is DIRECTLY applicable to our EventDeckSystem dialogue cards. Characters should have # handles. Dialogue should feel like someone posting their story online, not a neutral narrator.

### Quest Templates
1. **Data Snatch**: Get into X location, pull data from a terminal, get out before Ravenlocke arrives.
2. **Milk Run with a Twist**: Deliver cargo from A to B. Cargo turns out to be a living person/illegal sim. Now what?
3. **Smash Job**: Competitor corp/gang is using a specific facility. Destroy it. But there are people inside.
4. **Extraction**: Someone important is being held (or doesn't know they're in danger). Get them out. They may not want to come.
5. **Bodyguard**: Protect X while they do Y in hostile territory. X has their own agenda.
6. **Investigation**: Someone went missing. Follow the trail. Ends at a corp.
7. **Assassination (Wet Work)**: Mark has already been paid for. But once you dig, the picture gets complicated.
8. **Black Market Run**: Move restricted goods through a sector controlled by a gang you haven't met.
9. **Tag/Hypertag War**: A gang's digital tags are being deleted. Who's doing it, and why?
10. **AI Gone Wrong**: Something that was running the lights/security for a location has become... something else.

### Unique Encounter Idea: The Chrome Reaper
A gang of cyberware strippers who study marks, assess their augments, and execute precision takedowns. They use:
- Flashburst grenades to blind cybereyes
- Magnetic clamps on cyberlimbs to lock them
- TAP hacks to identify battery types and power systems before engaging
- They always know more about you than you know about them before the fight starts

### Unique NPC Concept: Dr. Meppen Type
A neutral "surgeon-king" running a clinic that every faction uses, giving them leverage over all sides. Their neutrality is enforced by the threat of "surgical complications." They're not evil — just practical. And desperate. And they're funding the whole thing through illegal means no one has found yet.

### The Megacorp Employee Who "Escaped"
Simulacra, Human 2.0, and android characters who have walked away from their factory-defined lives and ended up in the mall, trying to piece together an identity beyond their original programming. These make excellent recurring NPCs — people the player keeps running into who are figuring out what they are.

## 13. TONE CALIBRATION NOTES

IZ2.0's world is:
- **Not hopeless** — just relentlessly difficult. People laugh. They help each other. They fall in love. They make bad jokes. The darkness makes the sparks of community more meaningful.
- **Irreverent** — Characters swear. Corporate memos are satirized. Religious cults are lampooned. Nobody takes power seriously except the people who wield it.
- **Lived-in** — Every location has competing narratives, rumors, and opinions. No single "official" truth. Even the in-world encyclopedia has comment threads arguing about the facts.
- **Transhumanist** — The question "what does it mean to be human?" is live and contested. Protesters outside vat factories. AIs that feel. Humans who chose metal. This is not resolved.
- **Cynical about institutions, warm about individuals** — The corps are evil. The cops are mostly corrupt. But the street doc who charges 50% upfront is also the one who gives free surgeries to people who dissuade the gang from shaking her down. People are complicated.

This is our game's tone too. The mall is a ruin of a corporate promise, but the people inside it are real. They're angry, they're funny, they're survivors.
